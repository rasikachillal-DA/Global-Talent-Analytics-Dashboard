# Global-Talent-Analytics-Dashboard
**End-to-End Recruitment Funnel & Talent Engagement Analytics**

---

## 📌 Executive Summary

This project analyzes recruitment funnel performance, candidate engagement, and training effectiveness across global regions (AMER, EMEA, APAC).

The dashboards provide insights into:
- Application funnel efficiency (Impressions → Views → Apply Clicks → Conversion)
- Recruiter outreach and candidate engagement
- Learning & training completion performance

The goal is to identify **conversion drop-offs, regional inefficiencies, and engagement gaps**, enabling data-driven decisions to improve hiring outcomes and recruiter productivity.

# 📈 1. Recruitment Funnel Performance Dashboard

## 🔍 Business Questions
- Which function drives the highest applications and impressions?
- Where are candidates dropping off in the funnel?
- Which region contributes most to traffic and conversions?
- How does performance vary across quarters?
- Which functions underperform despite high impressions?

## 📌 Key KPIs
- Total Impressions: **635M**
- Total Views: **61M**
- Total Apply Clicks: **3M**
- Conversion Rate: **5%**
- Views by Function
- Apply Clicks by Function
- Regional Contribution

## 💡 Key Insights
- Sales drives the highest impressions, views, and clicks
- Conversion rate is low (5%) indicating major funnel leakage
- AMER contributes the highest share (~43%)
- APAC and EMEA underperform in engagement efficiency
- Significant drop-off between views and apply clicks
- Q2 shows better performance compared to Q4

## 🚀 Business Recommendations
- Optimize job descriptions and CTAs to improve conversion
- A/B test landing pages for high-impression roles
- Reallocate budget to high-performing regions
- Improve targeting for low-performing functions
- Introduce personalized job recommendations

# 💬 2. Candidate Engagement & Messaging Dashboard

## 🔍 Business Questions
- How effective is recruiter outreach?
- Which region has the highest engagement rate?
- What are acceptance vs decline trends?
- Are recruiters sending too many low-quality messages?
- Which region has the highest platform activity?

## 📌 Key KPIs
- Searches Performed: **14K**
- Profiles Viewed: **140K**
- Messages Sent: **32K**
- Messages Accepted: **9K**
- Messages Declined: **3K**
- Acceptance Rate by Region
- Decline Rate by Region
- Daily Logins

## 💡 Key Insights
- Large drop-off from profile views to messages sent
- Moderate acceptance rate (~30–45%)
- EMEA shows highest decline rate (~53%)
- AMER has strongest engagement and activity
- Recruiters are over-searching but under-converting
- Messaging lacks personalization

## 🚀 Business Recommendations
- Improve candidate targeting using filters and analytics
- Personalize recruiter outreach messages
- Train recruiters on conversion-focused communication
- Reduce irrelevant outreach to lower decline rates
- Implement candidate scoring models

# 🎓 3. Learning & Training Effectiveness Dashboard

## 🔍 Business Questions
- Which training types are most effective?
- What is the completion vs in-progress ratio?
- Which region is lagging in training adoption?
- Are training programs aligned with performance needs?
- How does completion vary across quarters?

## 📌 Key KPIs
- Completed Trainings: **341**
- In Progress: **34**
- Completion Equivalent: **6**
- Completion by Region
- Completion by Training Type
- Completion Trend by Quarter

## 💡 Key Insights
- AMER dominates completion (~93%)
- EMEA shows low participation
- Tip Sheets are the most effective training format
- Decline in completion from Q2 to Q1
- Reports are the least effective format

## 🚀 Business Recommendations
- Scale high-performing formats (Tip Sheets)
- Redesign or remove low-performing formats (Reports)
- Introduce region-specific training strategies
- Track training impact on recruiter performance
- Build structured learning paths

## 🧾 Conclusion

This project highlights key inefficiencies across the recruitment lifecycle:

- Strong top-of-funnel performance but weak conversion rates  
- High recruiter activity with low engagement efficiency  
- Uneven training adoption across regions  

Addressing these gaps can significantly improve hiring outcomes, recruiter productivity, and overall talent acquisition performance.
